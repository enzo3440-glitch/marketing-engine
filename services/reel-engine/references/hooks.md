# Hooks

The hook is the only thing that matters in a test batch. Write 6-10 per batch, tag each with a
framework, and test them on identical edits.

## Frameworks
| id | Framework | Shape | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| F1 | Contrarian | flip a travel norm | "Stop reading top 10 lists." |
| F2 | Insider | locals / secret / nobody | "The spot locals send their friends, not you" |
| F3 | Specific number | count + place | "3 places in town Google won't rank" |
| F4 | POV | first-person moment | "POV: you skipped the itinerary" |
| F5 | Mistake | warn the viewer | "You're doing Oʻahu wrong if you only…" |
| F6 | Curiosity gap | withhold the answer | "Found this by accident and I'm not saying where" (answer in comments = engagement) |
| F7 | Identity | traveler vs tourist | "Tourists go to Waikīkī. Travelers go here." |
| F8 | Story open | the trip that went wrong | "The best day of my trip wasn't planned" |
| F9 | Challenge | dare | "Find this place without Google Maps" |
| F10 | Question | answerable, divisive | "Would you hike 40 min for this view?" |

| F11 | Gatekeep confession | playful "I shouldn't share this" | "I probably shouldn't be posting this." |
| F12 | Stumble / we found | first-person discovery, past tense | "We stumbled into a micro jazz club down a side alley." |
| F13 | Best parts | the trip's best moments weren't planned | "The best parts of a trip aren't on the itinerary." |
| F14 | Walked past it | the thing everyone misses | "You walked past it. We didn't." |
| F15 | Nobody tells you | insider fact about a known place | "Nobody tells you Barcelona starts at 11pm." |
| F16 | Call-out / roast | gently roast a traveler habit, then flip it | "Do you plan every second of a trip?" → "Alright nerd." |
| F17 | Cost contrast | cheap vs expensive version of the same day | "This costs €14. The one everyone photographs costs €90." |

## What has actually worked
Record your own evidence here as the learn loop scores hooks (hook framework, views, follows, cost per follow).
Use those weights when a batch has to pick hooks; keep ~30% of slots for untested ideas. Avoid optimising for clicks
alone: check that clicks turn into follows.

## Rules
- ≤ 9 words on screen; say it in the caption's first line differently.
- Concrete nouns beat adjectives. No "hidden gem" (overused, and a place has to earn it).
- Match hook to first shot: the shot has to pay off the claim within 2s.
- Talking formats: the spoken first sentence IS the hook; trim everything before it.
- Never fake facts, ratings, or numbers. Never imply something is secret if it's a ticketed attraction.
- F11/F12 are first-person: only use them when the story is true for whoever posts it (gatekeep the *app*,
  not a public venue).
