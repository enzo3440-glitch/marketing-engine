# Music cleared for paid ads

No ad tracks ship with this repo (licensing). Add your own mp3, wav or m4a files to this folder and /brag picks from
them for every product ad. When the folder is empty, /brag picks from its own bundled music using the brief in
knowledge/brand-voice.md (Music).

## What belongs here
Music that matches your brand's feel and the ad's story: a warm cinematic bed, an indie or chill electronic track with
a lift, or a slow build that opens up on the payoff. Avoid generic corporate beds, comedy stings and trending songs.

## Licence: only add a track if it's cleared for paid social ads
- **Meta Sound Collection** (Meta Business Suite > Content > Sound Collection): free and cleared for ads, but only
  on Facebook and Instagram. Not for TikTok or YouTube.
- **Pixabay Music**: free for commercial use with no credit required. Its terms still advise checking third-party
  rights for product promotion, so read the track page before an ad runs.
- **Paid libraries** (Epidemic Sound, Artlist, Musicbed): strong catalogues; check that your plan covers paid ads.

Keep a line per track: file, source, link, licence, and the kind of ad it suits.
