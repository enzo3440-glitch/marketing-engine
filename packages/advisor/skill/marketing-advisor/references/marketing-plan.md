# Content pillars, weekly plan and launch rounds

Source of truth: `packages/db/src/marketing-plan.ts`; the desk shows it on the Calendar tab. The plan shipped with the repo is a small example for a neutral "Acme" brand. During setup the user's AI rewrites it (and this file) with the real plan.

## Pillars (example)

- **Point of view**: the usual way isn't the only way. Identity and independence.
- **Stories**: follow real people and moments that show why the brand exists.
- **Product proof**: show it working with a real screen recording and one next step.
- **Light humor**: poke fun at the problem, not the customer. Affectionate, then a real solution.
- **Challenge & discovery**: quizzes and riddles. Participation, not just viewing.
- **Weekly series**: a recurring editorial series that earns follows and saves.

## Every week (example)

- **Mon**: Instagram ad: launch-round ads go live / refresh; Instagram: weekly series episode
- **Tue**: TikTok: light humor
- **Wed**: Instagram: story post
- **Thu**: Instagram ad: matched variant or follow-up; Instagram: product in real use; TikTok: quiz / riddle
- **Sat**: TikTok: prove it, to-camera demo
- **Sun**: Weekly results review

## Launch rounds (example)

- **Week 0 · Prep**: can every test be measured and shipped honestly? Every ad has a creative ID, hypothesis, audience, destination, one CTA, spend cap, window and winner rule.
- **Round 1 · What makes it useful?** (weeks 1–2): plain problem vs proof vs humor. Judge cost per activated user, not cost per install. Small samples are directional.
- **Scale what activates** (weeks 3–4): second execution of each winner before treating it as a finding; scale only after downstream behavior confirms it.
