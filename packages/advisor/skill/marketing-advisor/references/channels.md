# Channel decision framework

Public Instagram: discovery feeling, single-shot/overlay, cinematic, owner commentary and useful product detail. Test follows per reached account and qualified downstream app use where attribution exists.
Instagram ad-only: truthful problem→mechanism→proof→CTA. Test attributed install/activation cost and retained-user quality, not cheap link clicks. Use genuine testimonials and licensed audio. The account identity remains visible; brand standards still apply.
TikTok: native-feeling owner-led/behavioral humor and discovery payoffs. Test retention/sends where authorized and actual acquisition; avoid assuming cinematic is bad or talking-head is automatically good.
Google: distinguish high-intent Search, app acquisition and website/display placements; require conversion instrumentation and compatible inventory/creative. Do not equate display clicks with app installs.
Partnership placements: hypothesis for relevant audience distribution. Confirm current ad/partnership product, geography, minimum spend, placement and measurement with official sources before recommending a buy. No assumed self-serve API or inventory access.

Evidence sources checked:
- TikTok Research API eligibility/approval requirements: https://developers.tiktok.com/products/research-api/ and https://developers.tiktok.com/docs/en/research-api-faq . Do not assume a commercial startup has Research API access; use an eligible authorized product/provider and inspect current terms.
- Google app campaign measurement checklist: https://services.google.com/fh/files/misc/app_campaign_for_installs_checklist.pdf . Conversion tracking and post-install outcomes matter; no universal budget or performance promise.
- Instagram recommendation engineering, background only: https://engineering.fb.com/2025/05/21/production-engineering/journey-to-1000-models-scaling-instagrams-recommendation-system/ . Do not infer current causal ranking weights from public counts.

This is a decision framework, not a claim to have read every marketing book or know all current trends. Refresh source dates and verify product-specific facts when making a consequential recommendation.
