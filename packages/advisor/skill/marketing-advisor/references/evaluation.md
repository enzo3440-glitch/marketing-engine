# Evaluation and learning

Editorial rubric v1: hook, clarity, discovery payoff, brand fit and action bridge, each0–4 with an evidence-backed rationale. Equal weights are a transparent design choice, NOT learned conversion weights. Never compare idea-priority scores with post-performance scores. Rank only like-stage items; preserve the score date, rubric version, source IDs, review depth and exact creative version.

Video review: host reviewer/authorized multimodal model must inspect the whole video and audio, including opening, pacing, overlays, context and CTA. Mark frame/time spans and audio depth. A user assertion of full inspection must not be silently generated from a filename. The engine's completion flag is an attestation boundary; production hosts must enforce trusted reviewer provenance.

Momentum uses measured view deltas over known intervals. Same creative, metric, scope, source and paid bucket are required. Rounded unchanged displays cannot prove zero growth. New posts, corrections and stale samples abstain. Do not combine likes/views/follows/install counts into a fake universal virality score.

Acquisition needs exact post/ad/creative IDs, reporting and attribution windows, spend/currency and install/activation events. Campaign-name matches are candidates, not joins. App Store totals do not prove post-level installs. Cross-platform attribution may overlap; do not add it blindly. Separate paid, organic, mixed and unknown cohorts, normalize age/format where relevant.

Feedback workflow: freeze score and creative version before publication; collect fixed24h/7d outcomes once windows mature; dedupe by creative; compare within goal, paid exposure, format and attribution regime. Hold out later campaigns from rubric tuning. Report counts, uncertainty and selection effects. Small samples warrant hypotheses, not fitted weights. `calibrate` provides descriptive holdout bins only, never automatically edits weights or trains a model. Require human review for a new rubric version and log rationale. Evaluate against simple baselines before claiming predictive improvement.

Review failed concepts separately from distribution failure: low reach/likes is not proof of bad footage. Identify missing inputs and propose a controlled edit/test rather than paying to rescue content by default.
