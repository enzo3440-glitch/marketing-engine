# Entertain the masses, educate the interested, sell to the committed

Source of truth for the ideas: `SLATE_IDEAS` in `packages/db/src/marketing-plan.ts`; the desk shows them on the Content board and Calendar, tagged by tier. Every idea is untested: it needs review, a low-cost test and approval in the desk.

## The three tiers

| Tier | Share of output | Where | Product shown? | Win signal |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Entertain the masses | ~60% | Public IG, TikTok, trial reels | No, or a one-second end card | Sends/shares per reach, follows, watch time |
| Educate the interested | ~30% | IG grid, carousels | As the tool you happen to use | Saves, profile visits, comments with answers |
| Sell to the committed | ~10% | Ad-only IG aimed at engagers | Real screens, one CTA | Store clicks, then installs and activation |

Each tier feeds the next. The sell ad should feel like the next episode of something the viewer already enjoyed, not a cold pitch. Retarget engagers (video viewers, profile engagers) when the ad builder supports it.

## Principles

- **Bold is allowed: aim at the feed, not the customer.** Critique the category's habits, never the people in it.
- **Show, don't preach.** Feeling-led reels usually earn better saves and shares per reach than lecturing captions and listicles. Open with the concrete thing and prove the take with a story immediately. Check this against your own account data before relying on it.
- **A payoff is not a hook.** Use the big emotional line as the end card.
- **Placement.** Keep the main grid for originals. Hot takes go to trial reels (non-followers only), ads and TikTok. Move a post to the grid only after it wins as a trial.
- **Lead offer:** pick the easiest-to-understand, easiest-to-want shipped feature first. Keep unshipped features out of ads.
- **Honest numbers only.** Never invent statistics.
- **Consent and rights.** Credit and get consent from anyone on camera; no invented "best" claims.

## Test order (Test Lab)

1. Trial-reel the entertain set; judge on sends and saves per reach from non-followers.
2. Run an ads-only screen on the sell set to the store; compare lead messages one variable at a time.
3. Promote winners to the grid. Re-cut the strongest entertain winner as the opening of the sell ad.
